Job Work 360 for Odoo 19

Complete Outsourced Manufacturing & Job Work Control Tower

Know where every kilo of your material is, what the job worker did with it, what it really cost and who approved every difference – material custody, vendor execution, production reporting, scrap, by-products, yield, quality, costing, reconciliation and compliance in one integrated Odoo workflow. Job Work 360 is a control tower built on top of Odoo's native subcontracting, inventory, purchase and accounting – it does not replace them.

Three pillars of control

Manufacturing Control

  • Material issue, consumption, scrap and by-products
  • Subcontractor stock with FIFO aging buckets
  • Customer-owned material kept out of your valuation
  • Multi-stage job chains with end-to-end traceability
  • Quality inspection, NCR and rework

Vendor Collaboration

  • Vendor portal for every job worker
  • Production reporting from the shop floor
  • Documents, delivery confirmation, material requests
  • Optional labour hours per operation
  • Approvals on everything the vendor reports

Financial & Compliance Control

  • Expected vs actual cost, including labour
  • Vendor bill variance control
  • Reason codes and approval for every write-off
  • Value held at job workers, by age
  • India: challan, E-Way Bill data, ITC-04

New in version 1.1

Reason codes & approval workflow

Every unexplained balance is closed with a reason code (weight difference, process loss, machine breakdown, defective raw material...). Waivers, high-severity items and chosen reasons go to a manager for approval, with optional write-off and vendor recovery.

Scrap & by-product receipt

Receive offcuts, turnings or returnable packaging together with the output. Expected by-products are proposed from the job type and posted to stock.

Subcontractor stock aging

FIFO age of every open balance in configurable buckets (0–30, 31–60, 61–90, >90 days), aging pivot, dashboard panel and automatic alerts for material held too long.

Customer-owned material

Job work done for a principal: the material is moved with the customer as stock owner, reserved only from the customer's stock and excluded from your material cost.

Multi-stage traceability

Chain view across all stages and vendors: cost, scrap value, lead time and final quantity, with a printable chain traceability report.

Vendor labour hours (optional)

Job workers log hours per operation from the portal; managers approve them and labour cost per unit is compared with the contracted rate.

Subcontractor Stock

Know exactly what material is lying with every job worker, its value and how long it has been there. A dedicated custody location is created per job worker under the subcontracting location.

Production & Scrap

Record actual output, consumption, process scrap (including returnable scrap such as brass turnings), shortages, rejection, rework and approved losses – all as real stock moves.

Material Reconciliation Engine

Unaccounted = Sent − Consumed − Returned − Scrap − Approved Loss. Shortage, excess scrap, low yield, over-consumption, overdue, invoice and quality exceptions are raised automatically with severity and tolerance settings.

Job Work Costing

Expected vs actual raw material, job-work charges, freight, inspection, scrap impact and other costs, cost per accepted unit and cost variance – in company currency, multi-currency aware.

Vendor Invoice Control

Bills are compared with the accepted quantity and the approved job rate. Quantity, rate and extra-charge variances are blocked until approved, put on hold or disputed – with audit trail.

Vendor Portal

Job workers accept or reject jobs, confirm material receipt, start, report production / scrap / rejection / rework, request material, upload certificates and delivery documents and submit completion – mobile friendly.

Quality & NCR

Job-level inspection with accepted, rejected, rework and scrap quantities, acceptance tolerance, automatic NCR and rework job creation. Works without Odoo Enterprise Quality.

Vendor Performance

Weighted scorecards (on-time, quality, yield, scrap, material variance, cost variance, responsiveness) with weights configurable per company, grades A–D.

Job Work Challan

Created automatically when material is dispatched (or on demand before dispatch and updated with the validated quantities). Principal and job worker GSTIN, state codes, HSN, UQC, quantity, value, GST rate, job work reference, vehicle / transporter, source and destination, and a QR code. Printable PDF.

Getting started

  1. Install Job Work 360 (it installs Manufacturing subcontracting, Purchase and Inventory if needed).
  2. Give users a role in Settings → Users → Job Work: Viewer (management, read-only), User (planners, stores, quality, accounts) or Administrator (approvals, configuration).
  3. Review tolerances and scorecard weights in Job Work → Configuration → Settings and the job types (machining, heat treatment, plating, painting, fabrication, rework are provided).
  4. Create a Job Work Order: job worker, job type, output product and quantity, materials to send, rate.
  5. Plan, Send to Job Worker (portal link), Issue Material (a standard transfer to the job worker location), then Receive Output, Inspect, Reconcile and Close.
  6. Optionally create the purchase order for processing charges: its received quantity follows the accepted quantity, so vendor bills are matched against approved work.

Good to know

  • Lots and serial numbers, partial dispatches, backorders and native returns are supported.
  • Multi-company (record rules on every model) and multi-currency costing.
  • A job can be started from a manufacturing order (Send for Job Work) and chained into multi-stage processing (Next Stage) with a common chain reference.
  • PDF reports: Job Work Order sheet and Reconciliation & Costing report.
  • Demo data reproduces a heat treatment → plating chain and a brass machining job.
  • India compliance (Job Work Challan, E-Way Bill JSON, ITC-04) is part of the module: switch it on in Job Work → Configuration → Settings → India Compliance. It prepares data only and does not file returns or call government APIs; validate periods and formats with your tax advisor.

Compatibility

Odoo 19.0 Community and Enterprise, on-premise and Odoo.sh. Depends only on standard modules: mrp_subcontracting, purchase_stock, portal.
