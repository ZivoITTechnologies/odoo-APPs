============================================
MOOWR Bonded Manufacturing for India Customs
============================================

Manage a customs bonded factory under Sections 58 and 65 of the Customs Act, 1962
and the Manufacture and Other Operations in Warehouse Regulations, 2019.

Features
========
* MOOWR licence with bonded warehouse locations, bond and customs station details.
* Into-bond bills of entry created from purchase orders, with BCD, SWS and IGST per line.
* Automatic matching of bonded receipts to confirmed bills of entry.
* First-in, first-out allocation of imported inputs to manufacturing orders.
* Duty content carried into finished goods and sub-assemblies.
* Automatic clearances for home consumption, export, scrap and supplier returns.
* Interest on goods cleared as such after the interest-free period.
* Protected bonded register, duty ledger analysis and bonded stock ageing.
* Monthly return as an Excel workbook and a PDF report, with reminders.

Configuration
=============
1. Assign the MOOWR User or Compliance Manager role to users.
2. Create the licence in MOOWR > Configuration > Licences.
3. Set the goods type and default duty rates on imported products (MOOWR tab).

Usage
=====
Confirm an import purchase order, click Create Bill of Entry, confirm the bill of entry
and receive the goods. Manufacturing, deliveries and scrap from bonded locations are
recorded automatically. Prepare the monthly return from MOOWR > Registers > Monthly Returns.
