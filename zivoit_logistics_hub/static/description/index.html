Why you need this module

Too many courier panels

Stop copying orders into Shiprocket, Delhivery and DTDC portals. Book every courier from the Odoo delivery order.

COD orders coming back

An RTO risk score with reasons flags risky Cash on Delivery orders before they ship, and can hold them for you.

Guessing the courier

AI ranks every courier by price, speed and the delivered and RTO rate it achieved on that PIN code in your own history.

Failed deliveries pile up

The NDR queue explains each failed attempt, suggests the next action and drafts the message to the customer.

Get started in 5 steps

  1. Install the app. It needs the Inventory, Sales and Delivery apps.
  2. Go to Logistics Hub, Configuration, Carrier Accounts. Add your couriers and click Test Connection.
  3. Create a delivery method of type "ZivoIT Logistics Hub" and choose AI Best or allocation rules.
  4. In Inventory, Configuration, Operation Types, Delivery Orders, enable "Print Label".
  5. Validate a delivery order: the courier is booked, the label is attached and tracking starts.

Try everything first with the built-in ZivoIT Sandbox Courier. It needs no credentials.
