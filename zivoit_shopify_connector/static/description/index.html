What makes it different

Smart Warehouse Routing

Rules by country, state, ZIP range or prefix propose a warehouse. Live stock decides. If none has it all, lines can split across warehouses.

AI Order Risk Hold

An explainable 0 to 100 score blends Shopify risk data with your own order history. Risky orders stop before confirmation, with the reasons listed.

Stock Drift Guard

A daily comparison of what Shopify shows against what your rules say it should show, with one-click or automatic correction.

AI Job Doctor

Every failed sync gets a root cause and a fix a functional user can follow. Temporary errors retry by themselves with increasing delays.

AI Product Matching

Connect an existing catalog without retyping SKUs. Barcode, SKU and title scoring suggest links for review. Only near-certain matches link alone.

Demand Forecast and Smart Buffer

Sales velocity, days of cover and reorder suggestions per variant. Optionally hold back fast sellers so you do not oversell between syncs.

Guided store setup

Setup in five steps

  1. In the Shopify Dev Dashboard, create an app for your store and install it.
  2. Grant the access scopes listed below, and request protected customer data access if Shopify asks for it.
  3. In Odoo open Shopify > Configuration > Connect a Store and enter your domain, Client ID and Client Secret.
  4. Map Shopify locations to warehouses on the store's Inventory tab.
  5. Choose your workflows and let the first import run from the queue.

Access scopes: read_products, write_products, read_inventory, write_inventory, read_locations, read_orders, write_orders, read_all_orders, read_customers, write_customers, read_merchant_managed_fulfillment_orders, write_merchant_managed_fulfillment_orders, read_assigned_fulfillment_orders, write_assigned_fulfillment_orders, read_returns, write_returns, read_order_edits, write_order_edits, read_shopify_payments_payouts, read_markets, read_publications, write_publications, read_translations, write_translations, read_locales.

Webhooks need your Odoo to be reachable over public HTTPS. Without that, the scheduled imports keep data in sync.
