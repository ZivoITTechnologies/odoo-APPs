# Changelog — ZivoIT Retail LMS AI

## 19.0.1.1.0
**New**
- Store Bulletins: launches, promotions, policies, planograms and recognition, sent to chosen regions, stores and roles; read & acknowledge tracking; overdue reminders to store managers; News tab in the learner portal.
- Gamification: 11 configurable badges with bonus points, Daily Boost streaks, a points ledger and a monthly Store Challenge (points per team member).
- Skills Matrix: skill targets per role; skill levels built from observations, role-plays and manager ratings; gap analysis.
- Career Ladder: next role, required journeys and minimum tenure; promotion-readiness score and "Ready for promotion" list.
- Coaching Observations: floor-walk checklists, 1-5 ratings mapped to skills, AI coaching plan.
- Instructor-led Training: classroom, virtual and on-the-floor sessions; calendar; auto-invite; attendance; learner feedback; new "Instructor-led Session" journey step.
- AI Learning Path Builder.
- Dashboard: bulletin acknowledgement, role skill fit, ready for promotion, coaching observations, badges, upcoming sessions, store challenge.

**Improved**
- Steps added to a live journey now reach everyone already on it.
- Role-play results feed the skills matrix.

## 19.0.1.0.0
- First release: role-based journeys with auto-assignment, product knowledge cards, AI Course Builder (17 languages), AI role-play coach, certifications with expiry and re-certification, Daily Boost spaced repetition, on-the-job sign-off, store KPIs and training-impact analysis, AI skill-gap and L&D advisor, Ask the Expert, OWL dashboard, mobile learner portal, brand context, and OpenAI / Claude / Gemini / OpenAI-compatible providers with an offline fallback.
